## Research objective and original problem

Biased gyroscope measurements stress physical filters and ML estimators differently, especially as bias patterns vary.

## Architecture and algorithmic design

Comparative experimental robustness analysis of rule-based and learned attitude estimation methods under gyroscope bias.

## Mathematical or information-theoretic formulation

Inject/observe omega_m=omega_true+b(t)+noise; compare tilt/angle error as bias increases. Exact test-bias distributions require full methods.

## Limitations and alternative interpretation
