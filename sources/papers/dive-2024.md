# DIVE: Deep Inertial-Only Velocity Aided Estimation for Quadrotors

**Reference:** Angad Bajwa; Charles Champagne Cossette; Mohammed Ayman Shalaby; James Richard Forbes. IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters 9(4), 2024, 2024. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/LRA.2024.3370006. Primary source: https://doi.org/10.1109/LRA.2024.3370006. 
**Applicability:** Transferable inertial estimation. **Evidence accessed:** publisher_abstract_only. **Code inspected:** none verified.
**Review cutoff:** 2026-11-13. **Independent replication:** no.

## Problem and motivation

A quadrotor can lose camera tracking and pure IMU double integration then produces rapidly diverging navigation estimates.

## Architecture / algorithm / rationale

Regress three-axis velocity from causal IMU history and use it as an update in an on-manifold extended Kalman filter.

## Mathematical or identifiability mechanism

The learned virtual velocity measurement supplies an innovation to a geometric filter; errors in gyro bias still propagate through its nominal attitude and velocity dynamics.

## Experimental evidence actually verified

Authors report 42% better localization on in-distribution flights, 22% OOD and 43% over dead reckoning in a VIO-failure scenario; no matched six-axis tilt benchmark.

## Limitations and critical interpretation

Trajectory metrics and flight distribution are different from tilt estimation. Full implementation was not inspected.

## Actionable connection to TG-DCM-PINN

Learned pseudo-measurement integrated into geometric filter, with an explicit measurement noise and testable innovation consistency.
