## Research objective and original problem

Scale, axis misalignment and static accelerometer bias distort gravity observation and sensor-frame alignment.

## Architecture and algorithmic design

Attitude-aided analytic calibration solving an affine accelerometer calibration and gravity reference using five arbitrarily oriented static known-attitude measurements and a constrained system.

## Mathematical or information-theoretic formulation

An affine calibration can be written f_cal=M(f_raw-b), combined with known sensor attitudes R_i and a constant gravity vector. Identifiability and minimal measurement count are tied to attitude availability.

## Evidence actually available

arXiv abstract and metadata report minimum five static orientations with known attitudes; full equations/experiments not independently extracted in this review.

## Limitations and alternative interpretation

Requires external/trusted attitude during calibration. Does not prove unaided online gravity extraction under arbitrary acceleration.

## Relevance and falsifiable transfer to TG-DCM-PINN

Improve pre-experiment sensor calibration with clearly recorded pose references; benchmark with and without calibration.
