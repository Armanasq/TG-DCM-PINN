# Theoretical error modeling and analysis of strapdown inertial navigation system alignment under zero-velocity conditions

**Year:** 2025. **Authors:** Hamed Mohammadkarimi; Mahdi Mobtaker; Mohammad Hossein Alizadeh. **Venue:** Scientific Reports 15, 41320 (2025). **Primary source:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-25054-9. **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-25054-9. **Access:** open_primary_full_html_and_xml. **Independent execution:** none.

## Research objective

Zero-velocity constraints and stationary alignment have orientation-dependent observability/precision limits that are hidden by idealized ZUPT narratives.

## Method, mathematical mechanism and assumptions

Analytical navigation error model and observability study under zero-velocity updates with simulations and known-sensor field/stand validation.

Linearized SINS error-state dynamics with velocity pseudo-observations; covariance and observability depend on IMU orientation, gyro/accel bias and sensor quality.

## What was actually verified

Open-access primary article and scholarly indexed text read; experimental results are author-reported.

## Limitations and hidden observation dependencies

Stationary zero-velocity periods, high-grade IMU conditions and calibration assumptions; does not demonstrate equal nonzero velocity closure across active motion.

## Relevance to proposed original research

Treat ZUPT+ESKF and calibration-dependent error bounds as mandatory close controls for interval-closure tilt.
