# iMoT: Inertial Motion Transformer for Inertial Navigation

**Reference:** Son Minh Nguyen; Duc Viet Le; Paul J. M. Havinga. AAAI 2025, 2025. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v39i6.32664. Primary source: https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v39i6.32664. arXiv: 2412.12190. 
**Applicability:** Transferable inertial estimation. **Evidence accessed:** paper_abstract_plus_publication. **Code inspected:** none verified.
**Review cutoff:** 2026-11-13. **Independent replication:** no.

## Problem and motivation

Long-range attention alone may obscure local motion transitions and desynchronized gyro/accelerometer interactions.

## Architecture / algorithm / rationale

Transformer with a Progressive Series Decoupler, adaptive positional encoding across inertial modalities, learned query motion particles, and dynamic particle scoring for segment-level velocity.

## Mathematical or identifiability mechanism

Attention and query-particle aggregation model multiple plausible motions, rather than maintaining only a single deterministic recurrent state.

## Experimental evidence actually verified

AAAI publication evaluated navigation/trajectory accuracy; GitHub publication listing observed but operational model code not verified.

## Limitations and critical interpretation

Non-causal/future-window characteristics and computational budget cannot be assumed compatible with instant tilt tracking; trajectory targets only.

## Actionable connection to TG-DCM-PINN

Treat multi-resolution temporal encoders as a controlled ablation; demand lookahead/latency budgets and same sensor channels.
